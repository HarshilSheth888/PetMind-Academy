# 🐾 PetMind Academy

> Expert pet psychology, guided training, and progress tracking, all in one mobile app.

PetMind Academy helps pet owners understand *why* their pets behave the way they do and turn that understanding into better training. Learn from expert-written articles, follow step-by-step training protocols, decode body language, and track each pet's development over time.

## ✨ Features

### 📖 Learn
- Featured **Mentality Guides** and a library of expert articles (e.g. *The Canine Emotional Spectrum: Reading Micro-Signals*, *Separation Distress vs. Boredom*, *Resource Guarding*)
- Articles tagged by category (Pet Psychology, Behavior & Fixes, Cognitive Development) and by pet type
- **Daily Behavior Fact** on the home screen
- Search and filter topics, plus bookmarking for later reading
- Personalized header for the selected pet

### 🏋️ Training
- Structured **Training Guides** with difficulty levels (Foundation, Intermediate, Behavioral Fix)
- Step-by-step protocols such as *Loose Leash Walking*, *Emergency Recall*, *Impulse Control: The "Leave It" Game*, and *Cooperative Care*
- Per-guide progress bars, recommended session counts, and a **Practice** mode

### 🔍 Decoder
- Body-language decoder that explains what a pet's posture or signal means (e.g. *Whale Eye*, *Play Bow*, *FRAPs*, tongue flicks and yawns)
- Signals grouped by emotional state (Defensive/Fear, Playful & Engaged, Alert & Assessing) and body area (eyes, tail, posture)
- Expandable **Actionable Dos & Don'ts** for each signal

### 📈 Progress
- Multiple **pet profiles** (name, species, breed, age, sex, personality traits, current focus)
- Choose from a set of avatars when adding a pet
- Dashboard stats: total training time, sessions, skills mastered, and badges
- **Skills**, **Milestones**, and **Logs** tabs, including session logs with reps, duration, star rating, and notes
